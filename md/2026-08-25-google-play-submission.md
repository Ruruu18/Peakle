## Store listing

**App name**

Peakle

**Short description**

Find courts, book games, join open play, and track your pickleball.

**Full description**

Peakle brings your pickleball life together in one place.

Discover nearby venues and open-play sessions, book courts, organize matches, and keep live scores. Build your player profile, follow your progress, connect with teams, and find players at your level.

With Peakle you can:

- Find nearby pickleball venues and available courts
- Book courts and review booking details
- Join and manage open-play sessions
- Set up matches and keep score, including optional voice scoring
- Track match activity, ratings, and competitive progress
- Create and join teams, chat, and coordinate games
- Discover players while controlling profile and location visibility
- Receive useful booking, match, team, and open-play notifications
- Report or block abusive users and manage your privacy choices
- Permanently delete your account from the app

Some venue, booking, payment, competition, and membership features depend on availability in your area. Location, microphone, photos, and notifications are optional and can be controlled in Android settings.
